This is the operational history, not a claim that every revision changed canonical scientific inventory. Source-of-record code is under `11_SOURCE_ARCHIVE`; R181+ research evidence is under `12_CASE_LIBRARY`.

- Built the 4-domain primitive inventory, Registry455 mechanism ontology, executable capability inventory, Foundry product universe and native search/selection/telemetry/quality infrastructure.

## R181-R200 — architecture growth slows; external-shell collision work dominates

- Repeated two-domain removal controls across selection, support, observability, semantic migration, conservation/identity, no-gold-standard validation, strategic evidence, endogenous rank and attestation.
- Results overwhelmingly collapsed to existing Registry455 compositions; canonical counts stayed unchanged.
- These rounds are retained as evidence/counterexamples and should not dictate the next frontier merely because they are most recent.

## R353-R372 — breakthrough-composition reset
- Switched from primitive/failure-shell elaboration to capability-scored non-additive compositions across real domains.
- R372 shift-aware option-preserving stack survived toy ablation and became a shadow candidate only.

## R373-R382 — empirical falsification
- Tested R372 on real market proxies and partial-feedback benchmarks. Full composition did not robustly beat strongest ablations; it was downgraded, not promoted.
- A later R382 coupled feasible-set idea remained only a next-candidate observation, not authority.

## R383 — control-plane restoration

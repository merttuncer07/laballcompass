# P017 RECS v0.1 — Relational Evidence-Conditioned Safeguards

## Composition

REL discrepancy localization -> record-level marginal culprit posterior -> CSID safeguard portfolio.

RECS lets an observed evidence inconsistency change which safeguard is economically justified, while CSID still prevents two safeguards that depend on the same evidence family from being counted as independent protection.

## Claim boundary

REL marginals are conditional on its enumerated non-empty culprit-set hypothesis space. They are not unconditional fraud probabilities. CSID receives additive per-record consequences; expectation remains valid under correlated culprit sets because the loss is additive.

# R-015 product result — PCBE v0.1

**Product route:** reconstructed standalone preferential-channel breakthrough estimator  
**Result:** working product; 4/4 tests pass

PCBE combines a widest-path topology test with a full resistor-network conductance solve. A channel
must reach the outlet and, when a baseline is supplied, produce the declared system-level jump.

In the first 20×40 construction, a straight 100-conductance channel had bottleneck 100 and tortuosity
1. Effective conductance rose from 0.51282 to 3.05128, a 5.95× jump. A control channel with one gap
failed the spanning test even though almost all of it remained high-conductance.

The product applies independently to porous flow, reactive infiltration, fracture, leakage, and
finite-network deployment.

# Authoritative user goal and boundaries

The separate lab's job is to use V2 to produce additional working products and useful product-product
compositions. It is exploratory and independent from the main lab: its findings supplement the main
lab, but do not silently overwrite the main lab's registry, evidence tiers, decisions, or current
frontier.

## Goal

Search the existing V2 product/capability space for mechanisms that can:

- form a standalone product;
- improve, protect, route, calibrate, finance, measure, or control another product;
- fill another product's declared weakness with a real strength;
- create a non-additive synthesis whose behavior cannot be reduced to running two products side by
  side;
- yield an executable mechanism, an observable result, or a practical decision advantage.

## Epistemological boundary

Academic consensus, novelty doctrine, peer-review status, patent collision, or disciplinary naming is
not a veto. External literature can supply mechanisms, counterexamples, datasets, mathematics, and
comparators. The decisive question is whether the declared mechanism works under explicit constraints.
Mathematics, executable invariants, contrastive controls, and real behavior constrain the claim.

This is not permission to invent evidence. Preserve the distinction between a hypothesis, a working
synthetic mechanism, held-out transfer, operational value, and deployment maturity. Lack of academic
scaffolding is not lack of rigor; rigor here means precise claims, executable tests, visible assumptions,
strong controls, and local failure maps.

## Failure rule

A failed benchmark, field trial, or comparator does not globally kill a product. Record the exact data,
shell, metric, comparator, costs, calibration state and failure mode. Then choose among repair, narrower
working region, router/fallback, component-only use, alternative field shell, more data, or an
inconclusive state. Initial application fields are test instruments, not permanent definitions of the
product.

## Productability rule

Score and investigate standalone productability separately from integration value. A capability does
not need to interact with a preselected list of products to deserve development. Conversely, a useful
component should remain addressable with its own provenance and boundaries after composition.

## Safety and field selection

Prefer early domains where outcomes are observable, interventions are recoverable, and failure is not
catastrophic. High-mortality settings can make every test appear worth doing and are poor first
discriminators of selectivity. A bad first use case diagnoses the shell before it diagnoses the product.

## Source authority

Treat every packaged result—including prior GPT conclusions—with a grain of salt. Read and run the code.
Preserve evidence tiers. Historical chats explain how the method evolved; they are not gospel and they
do not override the receiving user's current request.

# R400 frontier result

R400 tested whether the newly rehydrated V2P054 and V2P057 mechanisms support a distinct higher-order product under a shared acquisition/representation budget.

The first JARB prototype passed 13/13 direct tests and changed decisions in 53.3% of 1,000 deterministic shells, but it was rejected because it replaced the native REOC planner. The stricter JARB-Native prototype called the exact R399 V2P054 and V2P057 parent operators for every feasible budget split. It passed 13/13 direct tests; across 2,000 deterministic shells, 22.45% changed native decisions and the median declared-objective gain in changed shells was 0.1391. Miscalibrated consequence harmed 1.8% of shells.

The independent noisy-estimation/quantization transfer tournament was discriminating: with 100,000 common-random trials in each of 21 cells, JARB-Native won only 3 cells, all at the most extreme coarse-quantization step (3.0). The removal control won 18 cells. The mechanism therefore remains useful negative memory but is not promoted to V2P059.

R399 product authority remains unchanged at 57 executable suites / 44 distinct families.

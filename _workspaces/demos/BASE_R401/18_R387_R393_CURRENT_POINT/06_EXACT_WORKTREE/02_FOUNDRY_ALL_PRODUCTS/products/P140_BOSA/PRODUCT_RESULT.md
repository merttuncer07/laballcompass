# P140 BOSA — Burnout Overlap Support Audit

Parents: MBPF -> OWS.

MBPF's class-specific survivor counts are converted into initial-cohort-to-survivor importance weights. OWS then asks whether the original cohort still has adequate effective support for the late survivor population.

Benchmark: ESS fraction falls from 0.99588 at month 1 to 0.90312 at month 6, 0.78372 at month 12 and 0.66138 at month 24. With the declared 0.80 floor, the first fragile month is 12. High-prepayment class survival falls to about 2.02% by month 24. The homogeneous model's cumulative prepayment overprediction is 41.49%.

Claim boundary: this is a support/selection audit, not an estimator of causal mortgage effects.

# P136 QAIG — Quasineutrality Acquisition Information Gate

Parents: APTC -> AICC.

APTC's measured maximum relative charge imbalance becomes the Gaussian state around the quasineutral validity boundary. AICC opens an additional charge-imbalance measurement only when its expected downstream validity-decision improvement exceeds cost.

Benchmark: at imbalance 0.049 with tolerance 0.05, the precise channel has expected improvement 0.01312, net value +0.01112 and is selected. At imbalance 0.005, its net value is -0.002 and no channel is acquired.

Claim boundary: the Gaussian variance and measurement-noise model are declared uncertainty assumptions; APTC's transport status itself is not probabilisticized.

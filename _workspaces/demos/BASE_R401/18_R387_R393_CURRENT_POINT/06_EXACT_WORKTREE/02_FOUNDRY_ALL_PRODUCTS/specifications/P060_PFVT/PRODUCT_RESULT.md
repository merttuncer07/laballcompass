# P060 PFVT v0.1 — Protected-Financing Viability Tipping

## Composition
PRIU → TDSX. Each point on the declared funded-asset-value / promised-repayment surface is audited across every priority insertion. A point passes only when the same insertion both meets lender recovery requirements and leaves every existing claim no worse off.

## Benchmark result
At baseline funded asset value **100** and promised repayment **22**, the best combined protection/lender margin is **−2**, so no protected financing position exists. The nearest flip is funded asset value **105** with promised repayment unchanged at **22**, where the combined margin reaches **0** and protected positions appear. Changing repayment alone does not solve the protection constraint on the declared grid.

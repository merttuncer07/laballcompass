# Value-first benchmark batch 14

## RX-005 — CycleResidualLocalizer
Single-edge corruption is already easy for raw magnitude (AUC .989 vs cycle projection 1.0), so no
new product there. Coordinated cycle corruption is different: cycle projection AUC 1.0 vs raw
magnitude .675 with 100% top-k recall. Merge as a cycle-structured reconciliation extension.

## RX-053 — InformationProductionEquilibriumGuard
A fixed-production dissemination model catastrophically misses endogenous producer exit when copying
becomes easy: low-friction mean absolute error is ~597% of actual accessible information, and the
median largest one-grid-step producer-entry jump is 9. Low-cost-production control has no tipping.
Promote as information-market design core.

## RX-134 — EffectiveComplexityMeter

optimism variation and lowers optimism-prediction RMSE 2.186 -> 1.057 (-51.6%). Promote.

## RX-028 — TimescaleAwareDeadZone

nominal zero-risk threshold rule. Zero-noise control remains 0%. Promote.

# LABCOMPASS LCB-7F3A91 — direct-theory collision sweep r24

## Purpose

The prior queue used transfer utility and commercial/product novelty too interchangeably.
This pass applies a stricter rule:

> If the proposed product is essentially the canonical donor theorem/algorithm itself, or an already
> benchmarked core under a new receiving-domain name, transfer score may remain high but product score
> cannot remain 5/5.

## Result

All **69** rows that were still `LIVE` and `5/5` after the first r24 demotions were adjudicated.
Remaining raw `LIVE 5/5`: **0**.

Live product-score distribution after the sweep:
`{1: 21, 2: 65, 3: 15, 4: 64, 5: 5}`

This does **not** mean the demoted interactions are false. Most are scientifically correct and many are
useful engineering guardrails. It means they should not occupy the breakthrough frontier.

## Surviving score-3 wrappers

## Consequence

The queue no longer has any unadjudicated `LIVE 5/5` row.
The research frontier therefore changes from "benchmark the next 5/5 row" to:

1. re-open only score-3/4 applied wrappers where receiving-domain implementation may still be valuable;
2. mine the **full 455-family pair/composition surface again** using a novelty-aware candidate generator;
3. explicitly penalize same-theory restatements and existing-core children before they enter the curated queue.

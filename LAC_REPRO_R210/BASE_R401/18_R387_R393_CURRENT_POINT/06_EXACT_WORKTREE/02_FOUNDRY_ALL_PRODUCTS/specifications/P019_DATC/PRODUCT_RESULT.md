# P019 DATC v0.1 — Damage-Aware Trigger Calibrator

## Composition

DSDA sequential subcritical-damage evidence -> DTTC signal/threshold competition.

DATC lets accumulated evidence compete directly with the raw directed residual under explicit false-negative and false-positive costs. The selected channel and threshold are frozen before evaluation on a fresh sequence.

## Claim boundary

DATC does not infer the protected failure event or physical fatigue law. It calibrates a decision trigger over declared signals and labels. Synthetic benchmark results are not material-life validation.

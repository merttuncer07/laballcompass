# LGID v0.1 — Local Gain Incidence Decomposer

LGID turns local productivity/amenity capitalization into a standalone project and policy product.
It differences out a comparison area's common change, then separates:

- direct wage and amenity gains to residents;
- rent transferred by renters to landowners;
- renter net gain or loss;
- asset gains to owner-occupiers and absentee owners;
- the property-value component implied by recurring rent; and
- population response.

The calculator does not decide whether a local intervention is academically acceptable. It shows
who receives the real gain, who pays for it, and whether a headline neighborhood improvement leaves
renters worse off.

Run `python -m unittest -v test_lgid.py` and `python demo_incidence.py`.

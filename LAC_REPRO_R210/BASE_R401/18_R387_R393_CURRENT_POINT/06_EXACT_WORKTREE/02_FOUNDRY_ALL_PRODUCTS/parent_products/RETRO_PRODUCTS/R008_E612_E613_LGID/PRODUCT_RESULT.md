# R-008 product result — LGID v0.1

**Product route:** standalone local-gain incidence calculator  
**Result:** working product; 4/4 tests pass

LGID removes comparison-area trends and distributes a local wage/amenity gain among renters,
owner-occupiers, and absentee owners. It separates recurring direct benefit, rent transfer, asset
capitalization, unexplained property-price residual, and population response.

In the first construction, net monthly wage and amenity gains were 140 and 70 per household, while
rent increased 150. Thus 71.43% of the direct flow was capitalized into rent. Renters retained 720
per household per year, while the aggregate annual renter-to-landlord transfer was 5.4 million. The
measured property gain was 35,000 per unit, 1,000 below the perpetuity implied by rent.

LGID stands alone as a development, infrastructure, amenity, or local-productivity incidence product.

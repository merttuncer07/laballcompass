# Product Result — OWNM v0.1

**Retro candidate:** R-013 / C-376 / E-749–E-752  
**Historical decision:** `VARIANT_OF IM-008`  
**Product:** Ownership Wedge Network Mapper  
**Status:** independent working product

OWNM separates economic exposure from voting control. Cash-flow rights propagate linearly through
the ownership network; voting control propagates in rounds when a threshold is crossed and a newly
controlled company's downstream votes become commandable.

In the reproducible pyramid plus dual-class construction:

- the investor controlled OperatingCo through three 51% control links with only **13.2651%** ultimate
  economic exposure, a control-to-cash wedge of **7.54×**;
- the dual-class company was controlled with **10%** cash exposure and **60%** votes, a **10×** wedge;
- a cash-majority baseline identified only HoldCo, while OWNM identified all four controlled firms;
- controlled asset value was **2,100** against cash-at-risk value **331.681**, control leverage
  **6.33×**.

Four tests pass, including pyramid propagation, dual-class separation, below-threshold rejection,
and cross-ownership-cycle detection. This is an independent governance product.

v0.1 assumes deterministic known holdings and simple thresholds. The next standalone layer is
coalitions, board rules, shareholder agreements, convertible instruments, probabilistic voting,
jurisdiction, and time-varying ownership.

# Conversation Delta Reconstruction — R394 to Retirement

This is a compact reconstruction of the work after the R393 handoff. It is not a verbatim transcript.

- The user asked where the project should go next. The strategy was shifted to repeated diversity-batch adjudication of the R393 cluster frontier, plus continuing audits for invisible graph regions, followed eventually by empirical tournaments after executable composition saturation.
- R394 opened a first batch; multiple attractive pairs were rejected for collision or invalid semantics. ACRA→DSBC survived as V2P054 and was promoted.
- R395 again rejected a first batch, then DLEW→AICC survived as V2P055 and was promoted.
- R396 found and fixed a real product-level safety issue in the first SACPS→DSBC prototype: single beliefs could be uncertifiable. The contract was changed to fail closed; the resulting V2P056 was promoted.
- R397 first batch produced no acceptable product. A second batch found P017 RECS→LCB-K090; a separate miscalibration sweep was added specifically so declared risk would not be mistaken for truth. V2P057 was promoted.
- R398 required several diversity waves. Many candidates were rejected and persisted as negative knowledge. SACPS→ATRC eventually survived because ATRC itself could generate target-effect fingerprints without an invented bridge. V2P058 passed direct tests, sensitivity, exact-shell telemetry and candidate closure. Promotion regression had begun, but the instance was retired before all split state-sensitive batches returned terminal receipts.
- The final user instruction explicitly forbade another ordinary checkpoint and requested an extensive retirement ZIP in the same rehydration format. This package is that final stop.

## Strongest next material-product paths

1. **Decision Model Evaluation Workbench**
   - Compare decision policies under perturbation, cost and holdout conditions.
   - Produce a decision table and counterexamples, not another governance layer.
2. **Receivables-to-Liquidity Planner**
   - Turn invoice/receivable schedules into funding and collection actions under uncertainty.
   - Validate on an actual receivables CSV or a clearly frozen synthetic fixture.
3. **Safeguard Portfolio Designer**
   - Allocate a limited safeguard/inspection budget across failure modes and correlated exposures.
   - Report marginal protection, concentration and uncovered risk.
4. **Robust Model Shipper**
   - Package a model with drift tests, abstention/fallback behavior and reproducible inference.
   - Success is a deployable artifact that survives declared perturbations.

Do not bind these four into a grand architecture in advance. Choose the one exposed by the next real
problem, build it independently and retain removal controls and negative results.
